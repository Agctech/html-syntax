My journey into tech began back in 2023, when I got my first website.

At first, I thought having a website was all it took. I soon discovered that owning a website was only the beginning. Bugs started surfacing, things stopped working, and I had to pay a developer repeatedly to fix them.

For almost two years, I was afraid of what to do. I depended heavily on developers because I didn't understand the technical side of my own website.

In 2024, I finally made up my mind to start learning. I began watching YouTube tutorials and trying to understand how websites were built. However, after a few weeks, I eventually gave up.

Fast-forward to 2025, and the same problems continued. I kept dealing with website issues and still had to rely on developers. At that point, I realized that if I really wanted to become independent, I needed to take learning seriously.

So I decided to enroll in a college with a normal academic learning environment. I felt that having a structured environment, teachers, classmates, assignments, and deadlines would help me stay focused.

That was when I discovered SQI College of ICT.
I started my journey at SQI in August 2026. Today is September 1, 2026, and here I am building this webpage as part of my learning journey.
It may look like just a simple webpage, but to me, it represents something bigger. A few years ago, I was completely dependent on developers whenever something went wrong with my website. Today, I'm beginning to understand how websites are structured, how code works, how to use Git and GitHub, and how to put my projects online.
I know I still have a long way to go, but I'm excited about the journey ahead. I hope to see myself building more robust projects, becoming a better developer, and eventually being able to manage and improve my own websites without always needing to call a developer.

This is only the beginning of my journey into tech. 🚀
